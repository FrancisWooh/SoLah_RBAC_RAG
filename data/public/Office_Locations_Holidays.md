## Vietnam key holidays (Ho Chi Minh City office closed)

- New Year's Day: 1 January
- Tet (Lunar New Year): 5 working days around the lunar new year (mid-February 2026)
- Hung Kings Commemoration Day: 10th day of the 3rd lunar month
- Reunification Day: 30 April and International Labour Day: 1 May
- National Day: 2 September (plus adjacent day)

## Indonesia key holidays (Jakarta office closed)

- New Year's Day: 1 January
- Idul Fitri (Eid al-Fitr) and collective leave: around 20-24 March 2026
- Independence Day: 17 August
- Christmas Day: 25 December
- Nyepi (Balinese Day of Silence): March 2026

## Operational notes

Customer Support coverage on Singapore public holidays is provided by the Ho Chi Minh City and Jakarta teams for urgent tickets only (P1 and P2 priority). Payment cut-off times follow the banking calendars of the relevant clearing systems; FAST and PayNow operate on public holidays, while GIRO does not. For leave policy, see HR.
