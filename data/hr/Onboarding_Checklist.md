# New Hire Onboarding Checklist (Day -7 to Day 90)

- Document ID: H04
- Department: HR
- Owner: Amelia Koh, Head of HR Operations
- Version: v1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01
- Classification: Internal
- Compartments: hr

## Purpose

This checklist is used by the People team, the hiring manager and the IT/Security team to make sure every new employee of Aspire Demo Pte Ltd is ready to work on day 1 and fully ramped by day 90. HR owns the checklist; the hiring manager owns the items marked (M). Target onboarding satisfaction score: 4.5 out of 5.

## Pre-boarding: Day -7 to Day -1

- [ ] (HR) Signed employment contract returned and filed; offer conditions (references, background screening) cleared.
- [ ] (HR) Collect CPF, tax and bank details; for foreign hires, confirm Employment Pass or S Pass status with MOM.
- [ ] (IT) Order laptop and accessories; create email and SSO account (Day -3).
- [ ] (Security) Provision least-privilege access template for the role; schedule security briefing.
- [ ] (M) Prepare 30-60-90 day plan and nominate an onboarding buddy.
- [ ] (HR) Send welcome email with day 1 agenda, dress code and building access instructions.
- [ ] (Facilities) Prepare desk and access card.

## Day 1

- [ ] (HR) Welcome at 9:30, ID and document verification, building pass issued.
- [ ] (IT) Laptop handover, MFA enrolment and password manager setup.
- [ ] (HR) Company overview, values and Code of Conduct acknowledgement.
- [ ] (M) Team introduction, buddy lunch, review of 30-60-90 plan.
- [ ] (HR) Benefits enrolment overview, including medical insurance and leave entitlements pointer to the HR portal.

## Week 1

- [ ] (Compliance) Mandatory training: AML/CFT basics, PDPA and data handling, fraud awareness - complete within 5 working days.
- [ ] (Security) Information Security awareness training and phishing test.
- [ ] (M) Set 3 to 5 initial goals in the HR system.
- [ ] (HR) Payroll and expense system (Aspire Spend) access verified.
- [ ] (M) First one-on-one; weekly one-on-ones continue throughout the first 90 days.

## Day 30

- [ ] (M) 30-day check-in against the 30-60-90 plan.
- [ ] (HR) 30-day pulse survey; HR Business Partner holds a short call with the new hire.
- [ ] (Compliance) Role-specific training completed for regulated roles (KYC, transaction monitoring, MAS Notice requirements).
- [ ] (HR) Confirm all onboarding paperwork complete in the HR system.

## Day 60

- [ ] (M) 60-day review; adjust goals; identify learning needs and enrol in learning programme.
- [ ] (HR) Cross-team coffee chats: at least 3 colleagues outside the immediate team.
- [ ] (IT/Security) Access review: remove any access not required.

## Day 90

- [ ] (M) Probation review (standard probation is 3 months) with written outcome: confirm, extend up to 3 months, or end employment.
- [ ] (HR) Confirmation letter issued within 5 working days of a positive outcome.
- [ ] (HR) 90-day onboarding survey and feedback to the People team.
- [ ] (M) Set goals for the next performance cycle (mid-year June or year-end December).

## Escalation

Any item overdue by more than 5 working days is escalated by the HR Business Partner to the Head of Department. Overseas offices (Ho Chi Minh City and Jakarta) follow the same checklist, adjusted for local labour requirements and statutory registrations.
