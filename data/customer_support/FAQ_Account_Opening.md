This FAQ helps employees answer common customer questions about opening an Aspire business account. For detailed KYC requirements always refer to the CASA KYC procedure; do not promise approval outcomes.

## Eligibility and Documents

**Q1. Who can open an Aspire business account?**  
A Singapore-registered company (Pte Ltd, LLP, sole proprietorship or partnership) with a valid UEN and at least one director who is a Singapore resident or holds a valid pass. Foreign-incorporated companies may apply, subject to extra checks.

**Q2. What documents does a company need?**  
Generally: company registration documents (ACRA business profile), director and shareholder information, proof of business activity and recent bank statements, plus ID and address proof for directors and owners. Compliance decides whether anything additional is needed.

**Q3. Can directors use MyInfo?**  
Yes. Singpass MyInfo retrieval prefills personal details and reduces manual document uploads for Singapore residents.

**Q4. What if my customer's ID expires soon?**  
Identity documents that are close to expiry must be renewed before account opening can proceed. Advise the customer to renew first.

**Q5. Do you accept companies with overseas owners?**  
Yes, but beneficial owners are identified and checked, and extra documents may be requested, which may lengthen the timeline.

## Timelines

**Q6. How long does it take?**  
Typically 3 to 5 business days from submission of a complete application. Incomplete applications and enhanced due diligence cases take longer.

**Q7. Why is it taking longer than 5 business days?**  
Common reasons: missing documents, UBO verification, screening hits that need compliance review, or businesses in higher-risk industries. Employees should check the application in Aspire Core and raise a SupportDesk ticket rather than promise dates.

**Q8. Can we expedite an application?**  
There is no paid fast track. Account managers may flag complete applications to compliance once, and they are reviewed in queue order.

## Fees and Features

**Q9. What does it cost to open an account?**  
There is no account opening fee. Monthly plan fees depend on the pricing tier chosen (the Starter plan is free; paid tiers are listed in the current price list). Card issuance and FX fees apply separately.

**Q10. Is there a minimum balance or minimum deposit?**  
No minimum balance is required for the standard plan. A first deposit is needed to activate payments.

**Q11. Which currencies and payment methods are supported at launch?**  
SGD accounts with PayNow Corporate, FAST and GIRO, and multi-currency features for USD, EUR and others once the account is active.

**Q12. When can the customer get a corporate card?**  
After the account is active, the initial card limit is calculated by Aspire Core from account history. New accounts typically see a lower initial limit that grows with account activity.

## After Opening

**Q13. What if the application is rejected?**  
Aspire is not required to disclose all reasons. Direct the customer to the formal reply and log a SupportDesk ticket if they dispute it; complaints follow the Complaint Handling SOP.

**Q14. Where is customer data stored and how is it protected?**  
Personal data is handled under PDPA, with access restricted by role. Do not copy customer documents to personal devices or chat tools.

**Q15. Who do I contact for questions I cannot answer?**  
Post in Aspire Hub #account-opening-help or raise a SupportDesk ticket under Onboarding. Support is available 9:00-18:00 SGT, Monday to Friday.
